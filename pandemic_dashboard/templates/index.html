Interpretation & Limitations

SAFE means a completion ordering exists: each patient can obtain its remaining need (Need ≤ Work) and release its allocation.

UNSAFE means no such ordering exists under the current allocation state.

P(collapse) is estimated by Monte Carlo sampling: fraction of trials where admission fails or the state is unsafe. Wilson 95% confidence intervals are shown on the chart.

Limitations: Maximum demands are assumed known (Banker's requirement). Hospital capacity is simplified to five resource types. External epidemiological data is a workload signal, not hospital occupancy. Results are model-based, not clinical predictions.
